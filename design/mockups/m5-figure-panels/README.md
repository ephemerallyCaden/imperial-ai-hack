# M5 Figure panels
Concept: full-bleed simulated canvas plus an open drawer laid out as one journal figure (panels a-d, each captioned with units and n), and a three-scale zoom strip (x1, x10, x40) on the canvas.
Differs from the others: the drawer is the figure itself (ribbon, reliability diagram, booktabs scorecard, flag timeline); the dock holds only transport and a compact scrubber timeline.
Primary tone: medium (light and deep via `?tone=`; tones are illustrative muted derivations, not clinical colours).
Type: Charter/Iowan/Palatino serif for title and captions; SF/system sans for UI; ui-monospace for readouts.
Strengths: (1) captions and units make every number auditable; (2) reliability panel shows calibration at a glance; (3) zoom strip explains continuous zoom without a tutorial.
Risks: (1) drawer is 560 px wide and dense, so it covers a third of the screen; (2) four panels may read as a report, not a tool; (3) legend and zoom strip compete with the canvas for space.
All charts are hand-drawn inline SVG; all values and the reliability data are illustrative.

# M8 Technical
Concept: M7 (black figure panels) with the tissue redrawn as a flat hex-lattice simulation state, no gradients or organic blending.
Changes: discrete cells in flat fills (indigo intact, red-orange leading edge, magenta new tissue, pale-yellow dividing, near-black wound); um rulers on the top and left; registration crosses every 100 um; contours for t0 (6 h), forecast 90% closure and the +/-0.10 ensemble spread; velocity arrows on the leading edge; a probe crosshair with a site readout; a field-readout HUD (step, seed, n cells, area, front speed). Non-colour cues kept.
Renderer: design/shared/cellfield.tech.js (TechField). One tone only; no tone switcher.
Risks: more visual noise on the field; arrows and readouts are illustrative and not wired to a model; contrast not formally measured.

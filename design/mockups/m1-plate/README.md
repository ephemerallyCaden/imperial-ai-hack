# M1 Plate (primary tone: light)
Concept: a classic journal page, with warm-white paper, serif headings, hairline rules and numbered figure captions around a full-bleed simulated wound.
Differs from the others: the most conservative layout, with a fixed 320 px right panel, a masthead rule, a toolbar row above the canvas and a dock below it. No callouts, floating strips or lettered sub-panels.
Tone: light is primary. Tone colours are muted, illustrative derivations loosely anchored on Monk tones. `?tone=light|medium|deep` switches them.
Type: Charter / Iowan Old Style / Palatino serif for headings and captions; system sans for controls; ui-monospace for readouts. No web fonts.
Strengths: (1) reads as a figure plus caption, so a reviewer sees it as a paper; (2) hierarchy is clear: canvas, then ribbon, then panel; (3) the key, forecast and booktabs scorecard sit in one column, so the rules are always visible.
Risks: (1) the panel is dense at 320 px and the key text is 12 px; (2) the 148 px dock is taller than the 112 px in the brief, which costs canvas height; (3) the conservative look may not stand out in a hackathon demo.
Honesty chip, scale bar and the source line are always visible. The hatching and the "t0" label carry meaning without colour.
Everything on the canvas is simulated. The scorecard and forecast numbers are illustrative.

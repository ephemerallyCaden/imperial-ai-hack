# M6 "Night plate": extra mockup in a new aesthetic (14 Nov 2026)

Source: three reference images supplied by the user (not on disk; described here).
1. **HUD autopsy of a glass heart on a platter** (dark navy ground with a faint curved perspective grid): thin white leader lines from the specimen to small labelled callouts; condensed uppercase letter-spaced titles ("// AUTOPSY OF A MACHINE", "#Biomechanical Organ Analysis"); L-shaped corner brackets and a registration crosshair; segmented telemetry bars with amber numeric values; a dotted vertical ruler with a small triangular marker on the right edge; a small framed plot bottom-right; amber/orange accent only for numbers; restrained, no neon.
2. **Monochrome forensic collage** (black ground, greyscale macro imagery, wireframe/lattice overlays in thin white lines, many framed micro-panels with tiny type and registration marks, a concentric-circle dial).
3. **Vintage anatomical plates** (deep ink-navy ground ~#1B2038, white engraved line art, serif captions, hairline leader lines to labels, small numbered key lists, dense text columns).
Synthesis: a **dark anatomical plate / HUD** where the simulated tissue is the specimen on a dark stage. Still academic and rigorous: captions, units, n =, sources, "illustrative".

## Rules
- Use design/BRIEF.md for content, tokens (re-skinned for dark), colour rules and deliverables; the tissue colours and cues come from design/shared/cellfield.js unchanged (use opt.bg to set a dark backdrop only if needed, but keep the five state colours exactly).
- Content identical to M1 to M5 (honesty chip, scale bar, zoom readout, magnification ladder, minimap, Draw wound / Heal / Clear wound, two-option preset, collapsible panel with the state legend + rules + cues, forecast summary, mini scorecard, bottom dock with transport, one scrubber with t0 at 6 h, ribbon chart with 50% and 90% bands, source line, help affordance). NEW: a **tissue tone switcher (Light | Medium | Deep)** shown as a display setting in the header (it ships in the product; it only re-themes tissue colours, it is not a draw-tool control).
- Dark palette: ground #0E1224 to #151A30 (ink navy), panel #0B0E1B with 1px hairline #3A4160 and corner brackets in #E8EAF2, text #E8EAF2 (primary), #AEB4C8 (secondary), #7C84A0 (tertiary, only for non-essential text, keep >= 4.5:1), amber accent #E0A458 for numerals/readouts only, observed line white, predicted median #56B4E9 dashed, bands #56B4E9 at 18% and 30%, ensemble members #8EA0C8 at 35%, stall/uncertain flag text and hatch #B8A9E8 (light violet) plus words, focus ring 2px #56B4E9. Contrast: text 4.5:1 minimum, controls and graphics 3:1.
- Type (system fonts, no web fonts): condensed uppercase letter-spaced headings `"Avenir Next Condensed", "Helvetica Neue Condensed", "Arial Narrow", sans-serif` (weight 600, letter-spacing .12em, 12-14px); mono for readouts `ui-monospace, "SF Mono", Menlo, monospace`; small serif for caption/plate text `Charter, "Iowan Old Style", Georgia, serif` (13px); tabular numerals; never ALL-CAPS body text.
- Composition ideas to use: the simulated field shown as a **circular dish/platter** (clip the canvas to a circle with a thin white rim and a faint reflection/shadow ellipse below, grid floor in perspective or flat fine grid behind) relating to the scratch-assay dish; thin white **leader lines with numbered callouts** to a dividing cell, the leading edge, new tissue, wound and intact tissue (compute positions from the render), labels set like image 1 (title + small descriptor + amber detail); corner brackets around the dish and the main panels; a dotted vertical **magnification ruler** on the right edge with a marker (this is the magnification ladder: Cells / Colony / Wound / Field); segmented **telemetry bars** for P(close by 24 h) and ensemble spread; a thin **lattice wireframe overlay** option on the zoom inset (like image 2); a concentric-circle dial is optional decoration only if it carries data (for example calibration or P(stall)); otherwise skip.
- No glassmorphism or blur, no neon glow, no gradients beyond a subtle vignette, no emoji, no rounded cards, no shadows except the dish reflection. Everything is simulated: keep the honesty chip.
- Layout still full-bleed canvas with a collapsible 320 px panel and a bottom dock (about 112 to 150 px).
- Deliverables in design/mockups/m6-night-plate/: index.html (reads ?tone=), preview.png (medium), tone-light/medium/deep.png at 1440x900, README.md (max 12 lines: concept, differences from M1-M5, strengths, risks, contrast checks you actually ran). Render with Playwright Firefox (Python -I, scripts under /tmp/mockup-scripts/), look at the PNGs and iterate at least three times.

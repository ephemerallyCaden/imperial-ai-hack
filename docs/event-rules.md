# Event rules (verified 14 Nov 2026 from imperial-ai-hackathon.devpost.com/rules and imperialaihack.com/data.js)

- Teams of 1 to 4, student teams, valid luma ticket. Building begins 12:10 Sat 14 Nov; pre-existing projects are not eligible.
- Submit on Devpost: demo video up to 2 minutes + public GitHub repo. Submissions close 12:00 SHARP Sun 15 Nov (not 10:00). Video and last commit must be before 12:00; later work does not count.
- Building closes 21:00 Sat (no overnight hacking); doors reopen Sun 10:00 (per data.js). HACKATHON.md timeline assumes overnight work and a 10:00 deadline: needs correcting.
- One track per team; up to 2 bounties from own track. Figma and Adaption prizes are open to every track and do not count toward the 2 (show in your presentation how you qualify).
- Judging Sun 13:00 to 16:30: 10-minute slot = your 2-minute video, 6 minutes judge questions, then scoring. No slides and no live demo in this round. AI for Science judged in Blackett LT3. Track winners present on main stage 17:00, prizes 17:30.
  - Note: data.js says "No demo video needed: you present live". The Devpost rules say video + no live demo. Follow the Devpost rules; confirm with organisers.
- AI for Science marks (each criterion 0 to 3, weighted): scientific rigour 30, technical execution 25, path to the lab 20, novelty 15, video and answers 10.
- Bounties are judged on how central the partner's tool is. "Best use of Boltz": structure, binding affinity or molecule design (Boltz credits). Winners are checked afterwards for public repo, last commit before 12:00, and actual use of the partner tool.
- Other tracks' weights (for reference): Voice/Vision: experience 25, technical integration 25, creativity 20, usefulness 15, video and answers 15. Autonomous Agents: does it work 30, technical depth 25, usefulness 20, video and answers 15, originality 10 (from scout, not re-verified).
- Video structure suggested by organisers (from scout, verify on Devpost): first 15 s the problem and named user; ~75 s one real run of the whole task (carries most marks); ~20 s how built with bounty tools on screen; last 10 s closest existing product and how often it works. Label anything mocked or sped up.
